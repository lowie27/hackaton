From 2026 only fully electric company cars can be ordered. Employees with a fuel or hybrid car keep it until the end of the lease contract.

The benefit in kind is added to the monthly payslip and depends on the catalogue value and CO2 emission of the car. Private use and commuting are allowed; fuel or charging cards may not be used for a second vehicle.

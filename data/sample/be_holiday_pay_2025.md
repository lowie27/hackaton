White-collar employees in Belgium receive single holiday pay (their normal salary during leave) and double holiday pay, usually paid in May or June.

Double holiday pay is calculated as 92% of the gross monthly salary. When an employee leaves the company, the outstanding holiday pay is settled in the final pay slip as exit holiday pay.
